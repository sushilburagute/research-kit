# Roadmap

## P0 - research the author can use

- Establish the reader, question, tentative angle, scope, and capability limits.
- Investigate questions through source reading and iterative follow-up.
- Track claims, sources, upstream origins, dates, versions, and limitations.
- Search for counterevidence and distinguish unresolved from contradicted claims.
- Deliver an author-friendly brief, evidence ledger, and honest completion report.
- Preserve the author's narrative, notes, personal experience, and decisions.

Implemented: research instructions, references, templates, structural checks, and behavioral evaluation scenarios. The recorded limited-source trial does not establish live research performance.

## Portfolio workflow - implemented package

- Seven focused skills, including a coordinator and the original standalone researcher.
- Editable writing profile derived from selected samples and verified repository context.
- Outline/story composition, talking-point coverage, editorial decisions, and preserved author notes.
- Optional artifact/demonstration workflow with explicit assumptions, accessibility and static fallback.
- Repository discovery adapter for actual schemas, React/MDX or other content components, citation transforms, assets and checks.
- Draft claim audits and editorial/technical review, with corrections routed to the responsible stage.
- Persistent handoff helper: dependencies, scoped assignments, stale-result rejection, retry, revision invalidation, optional artifact skip and independent review verdicts.
- Sequential execution and host-supported optional subagents; no mandatory model runtime.

Implementation is instruction packages and deterministic handoff tooling, not proof of article quality, factual accuracy, or compatibility with every host. One [synthetic source-limited trial](../evals/workflow-forward-result.md) exercised host delegation, drafting, staged MDX and independent review; its final outcome remained limited because no renderer was available. See [the workflow](workflow.md) and [evaluation scenarios](../evals/workflow-scenarios.md).

## Next validation and follow-up

- Evaluate an actual article with readable primary evidence; audit citation support after integration.
- Run the portfolio's real content/build checks and rendered preview on an authorized article contribution.
- Compare usefulness and correction effort with a normal agent prompt.
- Test installation and workflow behavior across agent hosts, including an existing Obsidian integration.
- Validate structured evidence entries if observed errors justify a machine-readable evidence format.

## Deferred production and managed workflows

- Dedicated artifact-generation backends beyond host/repository capabilities.
- Talk structure, speaker notes, demos, and rehearsal material.
- Newsletter and social drafts derived from approved content.
- Advanced research backends and scheduling beyond host delegation.
- A custom Obsidian interface or standalone app when actual usage supports it.
- Managed publishing, scheduling, hosting, collaboration, and team services.

Choose subsequent work from observed failures on real articles. Compare against a normal agent prompt on evidence accuracy, missing counterarguments, corrections, and research the author had to repeat. Treat model scores as editorial signals, not factual accuracy certificates.
